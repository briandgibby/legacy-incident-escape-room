# ADR 0001: Product purpose and player agency

- Date: 2026-10-03
- Status at recording: Accepted
- Source: Explicit user direction for a fun, thought-provoking, educational game with memorable, unsettling lessons; the existing debugging and workplace-surveillance setting.
- Supersedes: None.

## Context

Legacy Incident Escape Room already combines a real debugging puzzle with an office controlled by Panopticon. A working technical exercise alone does not establish the intended game experience. The user wants players to enjoy investigating, think about what the workplace demands of them, and carry useful lessons away from the game.

## Decision

Use believable debugging, discoverable evidence, and meaningful player choices to connect learning with the escape-room story. Panopticon and the workplace should create pressure that players can interpret and respond to. Preserve player agency rather than treating obedience to the company's demands as the sole intended response.

Keep the PRD focused on purpose, audience, outcomes, and scope in plain language. Define concrete interactions and acceptance checks in the product specification. This record establishes purpose; exact escape mechanisms, timings, evidence objects, and difficulty rules require their own specification and authorization.

## Why and alternatives

A standalone coding quiz would lose the requested atmosphere and choices. A story that supplies answers would weaken investigation and learning. Combining evidence, technical work, and consequences gives the intended experience a coherent direction without prematurely choosing mechanics.

## Consequences

Features should support understandable choices and lessons through play. Memorable atmosphere and educational effectiveness remain hypotheses; implementation alone does not prove either. The helper should support reasoning while preserving the puzzle.

## Verification

Use play sessions and concise debriefs to learn whether players enjoyed investigating, understood their choices, and can explain a lesson afterward. Record observations and revise the living documents. Do not label these outcomes achieved without evidence.
