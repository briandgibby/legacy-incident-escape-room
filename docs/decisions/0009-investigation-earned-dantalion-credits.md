# ADR 0009: Investigation-earned Dantalion credits

- Date: 2026-10-03
- Status at recording: Accepted
- Source: Explicit user agreement that investigative tasks are a prerequisite for Dantalion credits.
- Supersedes: None; narrows task eligibility in ADR 0008.

## Context

ADR 0008 establishes Dantalion's limited fictional free plan and task-earned reset; investigation now becomes the earning prerequisite.

## Decision

Require investigative task completion before earning Dantalion credits or refreshing usage. Design the initial earning loop around investigation, rather than separate unrelated chores. Retain ADR 0008's brand and limited allowance; this record refines eligibility without choosing exact eligible interactions.

Quota, response cost, initial starter credits, cap versus accumulation, repeat eligibility, and specific digital or physical tasks remain undecided. The investigation prerequisite does not resolve these questions or approve timer-based replenishment. Track the refinement under G-13.

## Why and alternatives

Tying help to investigation can reward examining evidence and forming an understanding of the incident. Unrelated chores would disconnect earning help from the game's learning and mystery. Educational benefit remains a hypothesis, not an established result.

## Consequences

Preserve optional useful hints, manual deduction, independently viable repair/refusal routes, and puzzle-preserving help without finished-code solutions. Exact eligibility and pacing need specification before implementation. Do not imply that a particular physical task, replenishment cap, or farming rule is already accepted.

## Verification

When implemented, verify credits/reset require the selected investigative completion and that unrelated actions do not qualify. Check both routes remain viable without mandatory helper use. Record behavior separately from any claim about learning effectiveness.
