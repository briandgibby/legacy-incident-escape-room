# ADR 0014: Indubitably career pressure and candidate exposure ending

- Date: 2026-10-04
- Status at recording: Accepted narrative direction; exposure ending retained as a candidate
- Source: Explicit user direction for fictional career pressure through Indubitably and a user-envisioned exposure ending.
- Supersedes: None; elaborates ADRs 0001, 0005, and 0013.

## Context

Shift-based escape leaves the protagonist returning to an exploitative workplace. The user wants a reason that quitting feels costly and an envisioned conclusion whose successful exposure still carries personal consequences.

## Decision

Use Indubitably, a fictional LinkedIn/Indeed-style in-world app, to convey fear that quitting creates a black mark and damages employment prospects. This accepts career-pressure story direction, not a guaranteed career simulation or finalized quitting rules.

Retain this ending as a candidate: the protagonist delivers smoking-gun evidence of Rifkin's wrongdoing, including murders, to fictional authorities. The authorities successfully act and Rifkin closes. Hero publicity is followed by unemployment and rejection from other employers who similarly exploit staff in this fictional setting. Subtle doubt about the wider employment system lingers.

This is neither the sole nor finalized ending. Exact app interactivity, quitting options, chronology, evidence gates, and other endings remain unchosen.

## Why and alternatives

Career pressure connects remaining at Rifkin to the game's coercion. The candidate ending allows exposure to succeed while preserving unsettling consequences. An automatic resignation rule or predetermined unavoidable ending would overstate the accepted direction and reduce undecided player agency.

## Consequences

Keep in-world messages and authorities fictional; no public job APIs or real communications are authorized. Make no factual or legal claim about real employers. Preserve viable repair/refusal shifts and distinguish the candidate ending from approved completion mechanics.

## Verification

Review the app's proposed content for understandable pressure without implied finalized penalties. In ending proposals, verify authorities act successfully, closure and career consequences remain explicit, and alternative outcomes and player choices are not silently foreclosed.
