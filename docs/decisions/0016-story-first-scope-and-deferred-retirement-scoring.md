# ADR 0016: Story-first scope and deferred retirement scoring

- Date: 2026-10-04
- Status at recording: Accepted
- Source: Explicit user decision to defer Pirates-inspired retirement scoring for the current game, preserve the original authored story, and retain several endings and post-stage quitting.
- Supersedes: None; bounds current scope alongside ADRs 0014 and 0015.

## Context

A Pirates-inspired accomplishment/retirement assessment was discussed as a possible way to select epilogues. It remained a proposal rather than an accepted scoring contract. The user now prioritizes the authored story and the first playable level.

## Decision

Preserve the original authored story and previously accepted narrative direction. Keep the requirement for several endings and the option to quit after each completed shift/stage on either route.

Defer Pirates-inspired retirement scoring for the current game. Do not turn the deferred model into an implementation requirement, ending gate, or replacement for authored outcomes. Existing incident scoring is outside this decision's change scope.

Additional puzzle packs remain a conditional future idea, not a promised roadmap or authorized delivery. Their content and timing require a later decision.

## Why and alternatives

An additional retirement model would expand the current scope before the foundational level is complete. Staying with authored story and endings preserves the accepted direction while letting the first level establish the game experience. Deferral avoids treating exploratory inspiration as a committed system.

## Consequences

Remove the deferred model from current implementation prerequisites while keeping its proposal history intact. Preserve the candidate exposure ending, independent repair/refusal shifts, and distinct resignation choice. This record neither finalizes ending conditions nor authorizes broader campaign or puzzle-pack work.

## Verification

Review current requirements, plan slices, and continuation instructions for story-first scope. Confirm retirement scoring is deferred, puzzle packs are conditional, and accepted multiple endings and post-stage quitting remain explicit. No gameplay or scoring implementation is changed by this record.
