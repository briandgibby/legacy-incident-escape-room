# ADR 0013: Dystopian absurdism and shift-based escapes

- Date: 2026-10-04
- Status at recording: Accepted
- Source: Explicit user direction for dystopian absurdism and early escapes that earn the right to go home for the night, followed by the next workday's shift.
- Supersedes: None; clarifies ADRs 0001, 0004, and 0006.

## Context

Earlier decisions establish workplace pressure, viable repair/refusal routes, and a transition between missions. They do not define whether an early escape ends employment or only the current scenario. The user has clarified the campaign meaning.

## Decision

Use dystopian absurdism as the game's narrative tone. Each early escape earns permission to go home for the night. The next level takes place during the next workday's shift.

Both repair and refusal end the current shift. Refusal remains viable without repairing the incident; it does not mean resigning or permanently leaving the campaign. Preserve distinct consequential puzzle and crime evidence for each route.

Exact shift chronology, campaign length, transition presentation, and later ending mechanics remain unresolved. This record does not establish calendar simulation or select a final campaign outcome.

## Why and alternatives

Repeated shifts make a local escape meaningful while allowing the oppressive workplace and investigation to continue. Treating the first refusal as permanent departure would conflict with that progression. Absurd institutional demands can support the unsettling tone without turning every success into a final resolution.

## Consequences

Specifications and slices must distinguish shift completion from campaign completion. Preserve independent route viability and the accepted IT/account handoff. Do not silently turn refusal into resignation or require all evidence before allowing the player home.

## Verification

When implemented, check each early route reaches a shift-ending outcome and the next level resumes on the next workday. Confirm refusal requires no repair and does not terminate the campaign. Review tone through play; atmosphere and learning remain hypotheses.
