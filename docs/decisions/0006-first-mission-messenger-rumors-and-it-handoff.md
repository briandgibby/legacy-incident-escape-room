# ADR 0006: First-mission messenger rumors and IT handoff

- Date: 2026-10-03
- Status at recording: Accepted
- Source: Explicit user direction for employee-message suspicion, inherited accounts, and IT's second-mission workstation handoff.
- Supersedes: None; elaborates ADRs 0001, 0004, and 0005.

## Context

The accepted story concerns company retaliation, but players should first suspect wrongdoing. The current game has no employee messenger or inherited-account handoff.

## Decision

Seed suspicion with breadcrumbs in old employee-group messages about recent disappearances and unusual department turnover. Employees established the messenger, and it appears inaccessible to Panopticon. This apparent boundary does not establish guaranteed invisibility, encryption technology, or network-monitoring behavior.

In the first mission, the workstation remains logged into someone else's old accounts. Players encounter these messages before IT wipes the computer to prepare the second mission. IT then provides the player's own email access and credentials. All accounts, messages, and credentials are fictional in-game data, requiring no real messaging or email connector.

Exact thread wording, Panopticon's observation boundary, wipe disclosure, retention scope, notebook survival, and mission numbering relative to the tutorial remain unresolved. Track this capability as G-11.

## Why and alternatives

Rumors and inconsistencies build suspicion before stronger physical evidence. Starting with confirmed crimes would bypass discovery. The account handoff makes workplace control and changing access part of the story.

## Consequences

Do not replace physical refusal-route evidence with messages or make opposite-route records mandatory. Preserve independently viable repair/refusal escape and route-specific consequences. Technical implementation details remain proposals within the existing stack.

## Verification

When implemented, check the old-account messages precede the IT transition and new credentials follow it. Review whether messages seed suspicion without presenting rumors as proven murders or promising perfect surveillance immunity. Verify both routes still work independently.
