# ADR 0005: NDA onboarding and retaliatory murder evidence

- Date: 2026-10-03
- Status at recording: Accepted
- Source: Explicit user direction for an opening NDA/employment agreement and subtle physical-record clues about company murders of employees threatening NDA breach and public disclosure.
- Supersedes: None; elaborates ADRs 0001 and 0004.

## Context

The current game begins in the cubicle. NDA onboarding and murder-related physical records are not implemented. The user wants the physical-records/refusal route to reveal a deeper pattern behind the company's coercion.

## Decision

Establish as internal fictional story truth that the company murders employees who threaten to violate their NDAs and go public with what they know. Players encounter subtle physical records and contradictions suggesting this retaliation, without automatic murderer or important-clue labels or an upfront confession.

Begin the game with the player signing an in-world NDA and employment agreement before the normal tutorial and incident. Use polite, reassuring corporate language to conceal absurdly abusive terms that remain readable and understandable. This fictional action is not real player legal consent and requires no electronic-signature service.

Exact clauses, signing medium, decline behavior, and technical implementation remain unresolved. An onboarding state within the existing UI is a proposal, not an accepted architectural decision.

## Why and alternatives

The opener makes the company's coercion tangible before its consequences emerge. Re-reading reassuring wording alongside later contradictions can support recognition of the evidence pattern. An upfront confession would replace discovery with exposition; labels identifying critical clues would undermine the requested subtlety.

## Consequences

Preserve independently viable repair/refusal escape and their distinct consequential evidence. Gradual environmental clues should let players infer the murder pattern while retaining the chosen route's necessary clues. This adds narrative requirements, not a requirement to collect every record or follow the opposite route. Track onboarding as G-10 and expanded route evidence under G-01 in the living documents.

## Verification

When implemented, review the opening sequence, readability and corporate tone; confirm it precedes tutorial/incident play. Play through refusal records for discoverable contradictions and meaningful evidence consequences without upfront answers. Check that both escape routes remain independently viable.
